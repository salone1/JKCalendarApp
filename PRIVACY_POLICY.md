# Privacy Policy for Koshur Calendar

**Effective Date: April 24, 2026**

SA softs ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Koshur Calendar mobile application. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users.

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
Koshur Calendar is designed with user privacy as a priority. 
- **No Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts.
- **No Location Tracking**: The app does not track your real-time GPS location.

### 2. Permissions Required
The app requires the following permissions for specific technical reasons:
- **Internet Access**: Required to sync the latest moon sightings (Hijri dates) and official J&K Government holiday updates from our public GitHub repository.
- **Network State**: Required to check if your device is connected to the internet before attempting a sync.

### 3. Third-Party Services
- **GitHub**: The app fetches calendar configuration data from a public GitHub repository. GitHub may collect standard server logs as part of its operation, but no personal data from the app is sent to them.
- **UPI Apps**: If you choose to use the "Support Us" feature, you will be redirected to your device's installed UPI applications (like PhonePe, GPay, etc.). All payment processing is handled securely by those third-party providers.

### 4. Data Security
Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no risk of your personal information being compromised through our application.

### 5. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
